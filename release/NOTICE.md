Telemetry Change Guard is licensed under Apache License 2.0. The complete
license is included as `LICENSE` in this archive.

The SPDX and CycloneDX documents shipped beside each archive are generated by
the pinned Syft version recorded in `release-manifest.json`. They describe the
artifact Syft could identify; they do not replace vulnerability analysis or a
review of the source.

TCG is an alpha-stage deterministic safety engine. It analyzes configured,
supported evidence and can miss consumers that were not supplied or that use
unsupported syntax or systems. Required evidence that cannot be loaded is not
treated as safe. Optional AI features may explain or draft remediation, but
they never decide or override the engine result.

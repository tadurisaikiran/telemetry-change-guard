# Adopters

Organizations publicly using Telemetry Change Guard may add themselves through
a pull request.

| Organization | Usage | Public reference |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Adding an entry

An adopter pull request must:

- be submitted or explicitly approved by a representative authorized to speak
  for the organization;
- describe actual use, such as evaluation, required CI, or production-change
  review;
- link to a public reference controlled by the adopter when possible; and
- avoid confidential operational details.

Maintainers do not add organizations based on private conversations,
unverified reports, or their own assumptions. An entry does not imply an
endorsement, partnership, or support agreement. Logos are not used without
separate permission.

An adopter may update or remove its entry at any time through a pull request or
an issue from a verifiable representative.
